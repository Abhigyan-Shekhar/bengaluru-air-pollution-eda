04 / Method, provenance and limitations

Data lineage

Original: CPCB-derived Air Quality Data in India. Exact public copy: city_day.csv. Bengaluru records dated 1 January 2015 through 31 December 2016 are embedded verbatim, including missing fields.

Project: GitHub repository · Reference CSV outputs.

How the calculations work

Cleaning: parse dates and numeric columns, mark blanks and negative values missing, remove duplicate city-days. Groups: average observed AQI only. Pearson: pairwise complete observations. IQR: linearly interpolated 25th and 75th percentiles; flag outside Q1 − 1.5×IQR and Q3 + 1.5×IQR; retain readings.

Seasons: winter Dec–Feb; pre-monsoon Mar–May; monsoon Jun–Sep; post-monsoon Oct–Nov.

Limitations
Historical two-year extract; not current conditions. Daily city-level observations cannot locate hotspots or rush-hour effects. Missingness may be nonrandom. AQI is calculated from pollutant measurements, so correlation is not proof of causation or a specific source.
Future scope
Extend to 2015–2020, obtain station-level hourly data, join meteorology and road traffic, set coverage thresholds and test robustness on held-out periods. Independently verify the upstream source before formal publication.
